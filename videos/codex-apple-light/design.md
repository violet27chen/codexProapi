# Codex Apple Light Video Design

## Palette

- Canvas white: `#fbfbf8`
- Warm light gray: `#f7f7f4`
- Window gray: `#ececea`
- Border gray: `#d9d9d4`
- Primary text: `#1d1d1f`
- Secondary text: `#515154`
- Muted text: `#6e6e73`
- Focus blue: `#0066cc`
- Success green: `#0b6f3a`
- Success wash: `#e9f7ef`
- Completion wash: `#f0f7ff`

## Typography

- Display and UI: `Inter`, `Segoe UI`, sans-serif. This keeps the Apple-like spacing while letting HyperFrames embed deterministic fonts.
- Terminal: `Consolas`, `Courier New`, monospace.

## Motion

- Calm Apple-style product film pacing.
- Gentle window settle, precise line reveals, focus highlights, and quiet completion.
- Avoid black cyberpunk backgrounds, neon, glitch, scanlines, and aggressive shake.
